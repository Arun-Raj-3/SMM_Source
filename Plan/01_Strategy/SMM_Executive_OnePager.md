# SMM Platform — 1-Page Executive Brief

## What We’re Building
An AI-native social media marketing platform that connects content activity to measurable revenue outcomes, not just engagement metrics.

## Market Opportunity
- AI-driven social marketing is scaling rapidly.
- Most teams still cannot prove social ROI.
- Existing tools are fragmented across scheduling, analytics, CRM, influencer, and compliance.

## Core Problem
Current tools optimize execution (posting/reporting) but fail strategic outcomes:
- No true multi-touch revenue attribution
- No long-memory AI strategist
- Weak governance/compliance at scale
- Poor localization and cross-functional coordination

## Product Thesis
Win by combining:
1. **Agentic AI Strategist (NOVA)** with memory + autonomous campaign actions
2. **Revenue Attribution Engine** linking social touchpoints to CRM pipeline/revenue
3. **Full Content Studio** (text, image, video) + publishing + listening
4. **Predictive Intelligence** (pre-publish performance scoring)
5. **Enterprise Governance** (brand voice, approvals, compliance, auditability)

## Key Differentiators
- Long-memory AI strategist vs one-shot AI assistants
- Full-funnel dashboard (awareness → loyalty → revenue)
- Native CRM identity stitching and attribution
- Built-in influencer and community growth modules
- Multi-market localization engine with regional approvals

## Target Segments
- Solo creators (entry tier)
- SMB and growth-stage brands (core wedge)
- Agencies (multi-client operations)
- Enterprise teams (compliance + SSO + governance)

## Monetization
- Tiered SaaS plans (Starter → Enterprise)
- High-margin add-ons: attribution, localization, influencer intelligence, API, white-label reports, AI credits

## Go-To-Market Wedge
“Enterprise-grade intelligence at mid-market pricing”:
- Replace multiple disconnected tools
- Reduce operational overhead
- Improve ROI visibility for leadership

## Delivery Roadmap
- **Phase 1:** Core publishing, content studio, analytics, collaboration, inbox, CRM beta
- **Phase 2:** NOVA v1, attribution, predictive scoring, brand guardian
- **Phase 3:** Influencer, localization, video editor, community hub, compliance suite
- **Phase 4:** Enterprise ecosystem, autonomous NOVA v2, API marketplace

## Success Metrics
- Revenue influenced by social
- Pipeline contribution by campaign/platform
- Prediction accuracy and AI lift
- Time-to-publish reduction
- Expansion revenue from add-ons
