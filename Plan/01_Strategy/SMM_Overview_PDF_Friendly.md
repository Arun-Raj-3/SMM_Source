# Social Media Marketing Platform
## Product Overview

### 1. Vision
Build the only social media platform that connects content performance to closed revenue, powered by an agentic AI strategist and enterprise-grade governance.

### 2. Problem We Solve
Teams can schedule content and track engagement, but struggle to:
- prove ROI,
- connect social to CRM pipeline,
- keep brand/compliance consistency,
- scale across markets and teams.

### 3. Product Pillars
- Content Studio
- Publishing Engine
- Analytics & Reporting
- Revenue Bridge
- Agentic AI Core (NOVA)
- Team Workflow
- Compliance & Governance
- Influencer Intelligence
- Localization Engine
- Community Growth Hub

### 4. Unique Value
#### NOVA (Agentic AI Strategist)
Persistent memory, campaign planning, optimization, and autonomous execution.

#### Revenue Attribution Engine
Native multi-touch attribution with CRM sync and identity stitching.

#### Predictive Content Intelligence
Pre-publish scoring with actionable recommendations.

#### Brand Voice Guardian
Tone consistency enforcement with compliance-aware rewriting.

#### Full-Funnel Intelligence
Tracks awareness to loyalty and maps social actions to revenue impact.

### 5. Technology Direction
Frontend: React + TypeScript  
Backend: API-first Node architecture + relational/document data stores  
AI: LLM orchestration + predictive ML services + sentiment/voice models  
Infra: cloud-native containers, CI/CD, observability, secure enterprise auth  
Integrations: social platforms, CRM, analytics, e-commerce, collaboration tools

### 6. Commercial Model
#### Subscription Tiers
Starter, Growth, Professional, Agency, Enterprise

#### Add-On Revenue
Attribution, localization, influencer intelligence, API, white-label, AI credits

#### Positioning
Enterprise-grade capabilities at mid-market-accessible pricing.

### 7. Rollout Plan
- Phase 1: Foundation capabilities
- Phase 2: Intelligence and attribution core
- Phase 3: Scale modules and mobile
- Phase 4: Enterprise and partner ecosystem

### 8. Success Criteria
- Increased social-attributed pipeline and revenue
- Faster campaign production cycles
- Higher forecast accuracy for content performance
- Strong retention and expansion through add-ons

### 9. Strategic Outcome
This platform shifts social marketing from a content operations function into a measurable revenue growth engine.
