# SMM Investor QA Checklist
## Pre-Pitch Validation Checklist

Use this checklist 24 hours before pitch day and again 30 minutes before presenting.

---

## 1) Narrative and Message Quality
- [ ] Deck tells a clear story: Problem -> Solution -> Why Now -> Execution -> Ask.
- [ ] Each slide has one core takeaway.
- [ ] Claims are realistic and avoid unsupported hype.
- [ ] Competitive positioning is confident but credible.
- [ ] Closing message is strong and aligned to fundraising objective.

---

## 2) Content Accuracy
- [ ] Numbers in memo, brief, and deck are consistent.
- [ ] Sprint plan references match your latest execution files.
- [ ] Pricing and package descriptions are internally aligned.
- [ ] KPI definitions are clear and non-ambiguous.
- [ ] Any assumptions are explicitly labeled as assumptions.

---

## 3) Product and Technical Credibility
- [ ] Architecture slide matches documented platform modules.
- [ ] NOVA and attribution flows are technically feasible as presented.
- [ ] Dependencies and constraints are acknowledged where relevant.
- [ ] Risk slide includes concrete mitigation actions.
- [ ] Demo path reflects currently buildable workflows.

---

## 4) Investor Readiness
- [ ] "Why this, why now, why us" is answered in under 30 seconds.
- [ ] Business model and expansion logic are easy to understand.
- [ ] Use of funds is specific and execution-linked.
- [ ] Milestones are measurable and time-bounded.
- [ ] Ask (amount/use/outcome) is explicit.

---

## 5) Slide Design and Readability
- [ ] Consistent font sizes and alignment across slides.
- [ ] No overloaded slide (>5 bullets or dense paragraphs).
- [ ] Charts have labeled axes, units, and time windows.
- [ ] Visual hierarchy is clear (title > key points > details).
- [ ] Color usage is consistent (positive, warning, risk mapping).

---

## 6) Data and Evidence Quality
- [ ] Every key metric has a source note or internal basis.
- [ ] Revenue attribution logic is explainable in one sentence.
- [ ] Any benchmark comparison uses the same baseline period.
- [ ] Demo metrics and deck metrics do not conflict.
- [ ] Sensitive or private data is anonymized.

---

## 7) Demo Readiness (if presenting product flow)
- [ ] Demo account is preloaded with clean test data.
- [ ] Stable internet and backup hotspot are available.
- [ ] Browser tabs are prearranged in presentation order.
- [ ] Fallback screenshots are ready in case of live failure.
- [ ] 3-minute fallback demo script is prepared.

---

## 8) Q&A Preparedness
- [ ] Ready answer: "How are you different from Hootsuite/Buffer/Sprout?"
- [ ] Ready answer: "How accurate is your attribution model?"
- [ ] Ready answer: "Why will customers pay for this vs point tools?"
- [ ] Ready answer: "What are your go-to-market and CAC assumptions?"
- [ ] Ready answer: "What technical risks could slow execution?"

---

## 9) Team and Execution Confidence
- [ ] Team slide clearly maps skills to roadmap delivery.
- [ ] Ownership for each sprint/module is defined.
- [ ] Capacity and timeline assumptions are realistic.
- [ ] Known blockers have mitigation plans.
- [ ] Next 90-day priorities are explicit.

---

## 10) Logistics and Delivery
- [ ] Final deck exported in both PPT and PDF.
- [ ] File names are versioned and date-stamped.
- [ ] Backup copies are available offline.
- [ ] Presenter notes are included in your master deck.
- [ ] Opening and closing lines are rehearsed.

---

## 11) Rehearsal Scoring (Self-Check)
Rate each item from 1 (weak) to 5 (strong):
- [ ] Clarity of story: __/5
- [ ] Confidence in delivery: __/5
- [ ] Time control: __/5
- [ ] Q&A handling: __/5
- [ ] Technical credibility: __/5

**Target:** Average >= 4.0 before final presentation.

---

## 12) Final Go/No-Go Gate
- [ ] No critical content errors found.
- [ ] Narrative is coherent and time-bounded.
- [ ] Ask and milestones are investor-clear.
- [ ] Team is aligned on key answers.

If all are checked, deck is pitch-ready.
