## Slide 2 - The Problem
**Headline:** Social marketing is operationally mature, financially unproven.

**On-slide bullets:**
- Teams can schedule and publish, but cannot reliably prove social ROI.
- Analytics stop at engagement; leadership asks for pipeline and revenue impact.
- Marketers stitch data across multiple tools, creating delays and blind spots.
- Governance, localization, and compliance are handled manually at scale.

**Speaker note:**  
The market has many workflow tools, but very few outcome systems. This gap is exactly where we are positioned.

---

## Slide 3 - Market Opportunity
**Headline:** Large, growing demand for AI + accountable social growth.

**On-slide bullets:**
- AI adoption in marketing is accelerating across SMB, agency, and enterprise teams.
- Mid-market teams need enterprise-grade capabilities without enterprise pricing.
- Budget pressure is shifting demand from “engagement tools” to “revenue tools.”

**On-slide callout:**  
**Opportunity:** Become the system of record for social-to-revenue intelligence.

**Speaker note:**  
The opportunity is not just another publishing app. It is a category shift to revenue accountability.

---

## Slide 4 - Our Solution
**Headline:** One platform unifying execution, intelligence, and governance.

**On-slide bullets:**
1. **Operational Core:** Content studio, approvals, scheduling, publishing, inbox.
2. **Revenue Intelligence:** UTM tagging, session tracking, CRM sync, attribution dashboard.
3. **AI Layer:** NOVA strategist, predictive content score, brand voice guardrails.
4. **Scale Layer:** Localization, influencer intelligence, compliance, white-label reporting.

**Speaker note:**  
Our wedge starts with operational value, then expands into intelligence and governance modules that increase retention and ARPU.

---

## Slide 5 - Product Differentiation
**Headline:** Why we win against existing tools.

**On-slide bullets:**
- **NOVA Agentic Strategist:** Long-memory campaign planning vs one-shot AI prompts.
- **Native Attribution Engine:** Revenue traceability from post to CRM conversion.
- **Pre-Publish Intelligence:** Predictive score and recommendations before posting.
- **Brand + Compliance Guardrails:** Built-in enforcement, not manual review.
- **Agency Ready:** White-label reporting and client-safe views.

**Speaker note:**  
Competitors solve parts of the workflow. We connect the entire value chain.

---

## Slide 6 - Product Walkthrough
**Headline:** End-to-end workflow in under 5 minutes.

**On-slide flow:**
1. Create draft in Content Studio
2. Approve via role-based workflow
3. Schedule and publish across platform
4. Track attributed revenue in dashboard
5. Improve next post with predictive + brand recommendations
6. Export client-ready white-label report

**Speaker note:**  
Every step is connected. The user never needs to leave the platform to prove outcomes.

**Headline:** Mid-market wedge, enterprise expansion path.

**On-slide bullets:**
- Initial ICP: growth-stage brands, agencies, multi-brand marketing teams.
- Entry use case: publish faster with governance and visibility.
- Expansion triggers: prove revenue impact, add predictive and compliance modules.
- Sales motion: pilot-first onboarding, then multi-module subscription expansion.

We focus on segments that need enterprise outcomes but cannot adopt enterprise complexity and pricing.

Each sprint ships measurable user value and reduces platform risk.

## Slide 11 - Risks and Mitigation
**Headline:** Known risks with active controls.

**On-slide table content:**
- **External API volatility** -> connector abstraction, retries, fallback modes
- **Attribution trust risk** -> confidence scoring, auditable lineage, deterministic-first matching
- **AI consistency risk** -> prompt/model versioning, explainability, user override
- **Scope risk** -> P0-first sprint discipline, feature flags, controlled rollout

**Speaker note:**  
Risk is expected in AI and integrations. Our architecture and delivery method are designed to absorb it.
