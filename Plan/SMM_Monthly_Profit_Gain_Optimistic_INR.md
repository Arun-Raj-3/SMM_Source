# SMM Application - Monthly Profit Gain Overview
## 12-Month Financial Projection (Optimistic Scenario, INR)

---

## 1) Purpose
This optimistic scenario complements the base model and shows how faster growth, stronger plan mix, and better unit economics can accelerate monthly profit gain.

Currency is shown in Indian Rupees (INR).

---

## 2) Optimistic Scenario Assumptions

### Customer Growth
- Higher acquisition and better retention from stronger product-market fit.
- Active paid customers trajectory:
  - M1: 80 -> M12: 1,550

### Effective ARPA
- Higher Pro/Agency mix and stronger add-on attachment.
- Effective ARPA used: **₹7,600/account/month**

### Cost Structure
- **COGS:** 20% of revenue (improved infrastructure and AI cost efficiency)
- **Operating Expenses**
  - Month 1-3: `₹3,735,000/month`
  - Month 4-6: `₹4,316,000/month`
  - Month 7-9: `₹4,980,000/month`
  - Month 10-12: `₹5,644,000/month`

## 5) Key Insight
- Break-even is achieved in **Month 9** in this optimistic scenario.
- Profit scales rapidly from Month 10 onward as growth compounds and fixed OpEx is absorbed.
- By Month 12, monthly net profit reaches **₹3.78M**.

---

## 6) What Drives This Upside
1. Faster net customer growth with improved retention.
2. Higher ARPA through better plan mix (Growth/Pro/Agency).
3. Stronger add-on adoption and upsell motion.
4. Better AI and cloud efficiency reducing COGS to 20%.
