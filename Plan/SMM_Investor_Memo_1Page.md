# SMM Investor Memo (1 Page)
## Revenue-First Social Marketing Platform

### What We Are Building
An AI-native social media marketing platform that directly connects content execution to business outcomes (pipeline and revenue), not just likes and engagement.

### Why Now
- Social marketing software is mature on scheduling, weak on ROI proof.
- Most teams still cannot trace social efforts to revenue.
- AI adoption is accelerating, but current tools are mostly one-shot assistants without strategic memory.

### Core Product Thesis
Win by combining workflow, intelligence, and attribution in one system:
1. **Operational Core:** Create, approve, schedule, publish, and manage inbox in one place.
2. **Revenue Intelligence:** Native UTM, session tracking, CRM sync, and attribution dashboard.
3. **AI Advantage:** NOVA (long-memory campaign strategist), predictive content scoring, brand voice guardrails.
4. **Scale Layer:** Localization engine, influencer intelligence, compliance suite, white-label agency reporting.

### Differentiation
- **NOVA Agentic Strategist:** Persistent memory and goal-driven planning.
- **Native Attribution:** Multi-step conversion lineage from social touchpoint to CRM outcomes.
- **Pre-Publish Intelligence:** Predictive score and actionable recommendations.
- **Enterprise Controls:** Compliance + governance + auditability built-in.
- **Agency Readiness:** White-label reporting and client-safe workspace outputs.

### Business Model
- Tiered SaaS plans: Starter, Growth, Professional, Agency, Enterprise.
- Expansion revenue from add-ons: attribution, localization, influencer module, white-label, API, AI credits.
- Land-and-expand strategy increases ARPU as customers activate advanced modules.

### Execution Plan (8 Weeks)
- **Sprint 1:** Workflow MVP (`create -> approve -> schedule -> publish`)
- **Sprint 2:** NOVA v1 + Attribution MVP
- **Sprint 3:** Predictive scoring + Brand voice + Competitor benchmarks
- **Sprint 4:** Localization + Influencer + Compliance + White-label

### Key KPIs
- Attributed pipeline and revenue per campaign/platform
- Weekly active workspaces and feature adoption
- Publish success rate and time-to-publish reduction
- AI recommendation acceptance and confidence trends

### Risk and Mitigation
- **External API volatility:** Connector abstraction, retries, fallback modes.
- **Attribution trust risk:** Confidence-scored identity stitching and auditable lineage.
- **AI consistency risk:** Prompt/model versioning, explainability, and human override.
- **Scope risk:** P0-first delivery discipline and staged rollout via feature flags.

### Why This Wins
This is not another posting tool. It is a revenue operating system for social marketing: unifying execution, attribution, intelligence, and governance at a price point accessible to mid-market teams and expandable into enterprise.

### Immediate Milestones
1. Complete Sprint 1-4 roadmap.
2. Demonstrate attributed revenue in pilot accounts.
3. Convert pilots to paid multi-module subscriptions.
4. Scale enterprise controls and partner integrations.
