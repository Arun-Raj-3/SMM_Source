# SMM Investor Deck Design Guide
## Visual System and Presentation Standards

---

## 1) Design Objectives
- Communicate confidence, clarity, and execution readiness.
- Keep visuals clean and investor-friendly (low noise, high signal).
- Emphasize outcome metrics (revenue, execution speed, adoption) over feature clutter.

---

## 2) Brand Style Direction

### Theme
- **Style:** Modern B2B SaaS, data-driven, premium minimal.
- **Tone:** Strategic, credible, execution-focused.
- **Visual balance:** 70% clean whitespace, 30% data/graphics.

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** `#1F4BFF` (Trust/technology blue)
- **Secondary:** `#111827` (Deep slate for text)
- **Accent:** `#00B894` (Performance/positive KPI)
- **Warning:** `#F59E0B` (Risk/attention)
- **Negative:** `#EF4444` (Issue/downside)
- **Background:** `#F8FAFC` or white
- **Card background:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Border:** `#E5E7EB`

### Typography
- **Title font:** Inter / Poppins Semibold
- **Body font:** Inter / Source Sans Pro Regular
- **Sizes:**
  - Slide title: 34-40 pt
  - Section heading: 24-28 pt
  - Body text: 16-20 pt
  - Footnotes: 11-12 pt
- **Rule:** Max 2 font families in entire deck.

---

## 3) Slide Layout System

### Grid
- Use 12-column grid equivalent for alignment.
- Keep left and right margins consistent (5-7% of slide width).
- Maintain visual rhythm with repeated card widths and spacing.

### Default Layout Patterns
1. **Title + 3 bullets + visual**
2. **Two-column compare (problem vs solution)**
3. **KPI card row (3-4 cards)**
4. **Timeline strip (sprints/milestones)**
5. **Matrix/table (competition/risk)**

### Content Density Rules
- Max 5 bullets per slide.
- Max 8 words per bullet where possible.
- One key message per slide.
- Use build animations sparingly (fade only).

---

## 4) Visual Asset Guidance

### Icons
- Use one icon family only (outline or filled, not mixed).
- Suggested sets: Heroicons, Lucide, Material Symbols.
- Keep icon stroke/weight consistent across deck.

### Charts
- Prefer simple visuals:
  - Line chart for trend
  - Bar chart for comparison
  - Donut for allocation
- Avoid 3D charts and dense legends.
- Always label units and timeframe.

### Product Screens
- Use 2-3 clear screenshots max.
- Add short captions with outcome statement.
- Blur confidential data and credentials.

---

## 5) Slide-by-Slide Design Notes

### Slide 1 (Title)
- Large headline, subtle gradient background.
- Add product logo and short tagline.

### Slide 2 (Problem)
- Left: pain bullets.
- Right: fragmented toolchain diagram.

### Slide 3 (Market)
- Top: market headline.
- Bottom: single growth chart + key callout.

### Slide 4 (Solution)
- Four stacked cards (Operational, Revenue, AI, Scale).
- Use distinct icons and short one-line descriptors.

### Slide 5 (Differentiation)
- Competitor matrix with checkmarks.
- Highlight "Our Platform" column in primary color.

### Slide 6 (Workflow)
- Horizontal process flow with arrows.
- Numbered steps and compact labels.

### Slide 7 (Business Model)
- Pricing ladder + add-on wheel.
- Emphasize expansion logic visually.

### Slide 8 (GTM)
- Funnel style: ICP -> Pilot -> Conversion -> Expansion.

### Slide 9 (Execution Plan)
- 4-phase timeline with milestone markers.
- Show risk reduction and value increase per sprint.

### Slide 10 (KPIs)
- Use 4 metric cards with icons.
- Include 1 sample dashboard screenshot.

### Slide 11 (Risks)
- Two-column table: risk and mitigation.
- Use warning color only for headers, not full blocks.

### Slide 12 (Ask)
- Capital use pie chart + milestone timeline.
- Strong closing statement at bottom.

---

## 6) Data Storytelling Rules
- Lead with business outcomes, then feature evidence.
- Every metric needs:
  - definition,
  - time window,
  - source clarity.
- Use directional language:
  - "improve", "reduce", "increase", "accelerate".
- Avoid unverifiable superlatives unless cited.

---

## 7) Voice and Copy Standards
- Use active voice and concise phrasing.
- Replace jargon-heavy lines with outcome-driven statements.
- Preferred structure:
  - **Problem -> Impact -> Solution -> Result**
- Keep each slide headline as an insight, not a label.
  - Good: "Social teams lack revenue visibility"
  - Weak: "Problem Statement"

---

## 8) Consistency Checklist (Design)
- Same title position across all slides
- Same color mapping for positive/negative signals
- Same corner radius for all cards
- Same spacing scale (8px base rhythm equivalent)
- Same chart style and axis labeling approach
- No mixed icon packs or inconsistent shadows

---

## 9) Export and Delivery Settings
- Slide size: 16:9 widescreen
- Export formats:
  - Presentation (`.pptx` or Canva link)
  - PDF (for sharing)
- Embed fonts when exporting from PowerPoint.
- Test projection readability at 100% zoom on laptop and external display.

---

## 10) Final Recommended Workflow
1. Build deck with `Plan/SMM_Investor_Deck_Content.md`
2. Apply this design guide for consistency
3. Validate with `Plan/SMM_Investor_QA_Checklist.md`
4. Run one timed rehearsal (2-min + 10-min versions)
5. Export final pitch deck and backup PDF
